Privacy Policy

wildvoltaireian.xyz
Last updated: October 02, 2026

Thanks for checking out wildvoltaireian.xyz! I believe in keeping your data private and only collecting what is absolutely necessary. This privacy policy outlines what information I collect, how I use it, and your rights regarding your data.
Disclaimer: As an independent website, WildVoltaireian bears no direct affiliation with any wiki farm (such as Miraheze/WikiTide Foundation, Fandom, Inc.), nor with the video game developer Roblox Corporation. Any complaints or feedback regarding services provided by them should be directed to the relevant official feedback channels.

Information I Collect

  • Email: This is only if you choose to send an email to me on my contact page.
  • Server Logs: GitHub Pages automatically logs basic request information, such as IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting purposes.

How I Use Your Information

I only use your email (if you sent me an email through my contact page) to respond to inquiries.

Third-Party Services

I use a few trusted services to help run the site:

  • Web3Forms: To handle my contact form.
  • GitHub Pages: To host the site using Jekyll.
  • Cloudflare: To manage my domain. I also use a worker script to automatically fetch stats from all my wikis.

Your Rights (GDPR & Privacy)

Regardless of where you live, I recognize your rights to your own data:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of any data I have about you.
  • Right to Correction: If you believe any data I have about you is incorrect, you can request a correction.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request that I delete any personal data I have about you.

Children's Privacy

This site is not intended for children under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If I discover that a child under 13 has provided me with personal information, I will delete it immediately.
