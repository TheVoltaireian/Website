Wikis

I've been working on wikis since 2023. Here are some of my best works!

Zombies vs Humans

June 29th, 2026 - Present
The official Zombies vs Humans Wiki was my most recent project on Miraheze. Started on June 29, 2026 as a fork of the original Fandom wiki, I expanded and revamped much of the content originally on the wiki, such as:
  • A brand-new main page
  • Multiple revamped templates & modules
  • A brand new UI/UX experience
  • RobloxAPI & ZvH Lorebook integration
  • Project namespace with rules & guidelines
This wiki became official and replaced the Fandom wiki on August 7th, 2026.
Visit wiki WIP

10 Player Flee

March 28th, 2026 - Present
The official 10 Player Flee Wiki was one of my most challenging wikis. Started on March 28th, 2026 from the ground up, I created a new and modern information hub for 10 Player Flee, with:
  • A main page with many sections for readers & editors alike
  • Pages with advanced templates & modules
  • An accessible UI
  • Valuelist integration
  • Five areas of content divided into namespaces (WIP)
  • A cosmetic database (WIP)
I worked with many members of the community to ensure this could be the best resource available for 10 Player Flee.

The wiki launched in beta on May 24th, 2026. A full release is planned for December 2026.
Visit wiki WIP

Forsaken

September 2025 - February 2nd, 2026
The official Forsaken Wiki was one of the wikis I spent the most time on. I was brought onto the team as an interface administrator in September of 2025, and in my time I contributed to many of the wiki's pages and features, such as:
  • The main page design
  • The seasonal themes on the site (Halloween, Christmas, Anniversary)
  • The first Mbox template design
  • The policy pages
  • Also contributed to: Skin pages, infoboxes, staff name colors
Working on the Forsaken Wiki was a fun and creative experience, as it was my second time working on a non-Citizen wiki. I departed from the wiki team in February of 2026 to focus on my own mental health.
Visit wiki WIP

Guts & Blackpowder

March 8th, 2025 - Present
The official Guts & Blackpowder Wiki was the second Miraheze wiki I worked on after returning from my year-long break.

I was brought onto the team as a CSS Specialist in March of 2025, and I have helped out with refactoring some of the CSS on the site, and offering help whenever it is needed.
Visit wiki WIP

Peak Precision Wikis

January 2025 - August 2025 Sunsetted
Peak Precision Wikis was my first multi-wiki project, and also my first wiki on Miraheze. I covered the Roblox games Outlaster & Eviction Notice, created by Peak Precision Studios.

After a few months, I ended up sunsetting the wikis due to a decreasing lack of interest and distaste wwith how the community for the game was handled. I still appreaciate the experience as it gave me valuable skills and experience that I could apply to other projects.

Timebomb Madness

April 2024 - June 2024 Fandom Sunsetted
Timebomb Madness was my second-ever wiki project, and the first wiki I was a bureaucrat on. I worked on the wiki for this new build-to-survive experience on Roblox, Timebomb Madness. I worked on it from April to June of 2024, and departed from the team after general distaste in how the game was handled, and the wiki was shut down soon after.

Average Plate Gaem

October 2023 - June 2024 Fandom Sunsetted
Average Plate Gaem was my first-ever wiki project. I started out as a regular editor who frequently revamped and updated pages to fit the standards of the wiki, and I quickly rose up the ranks to Head Admin with 2,000+ edits. I contributed greatly to the wiki for several months, and eventually decided to leave in June to go on my own path. The wiki was eventually forked to Miraheze.
